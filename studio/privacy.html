Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Draft. This policy describes how iNardi works today. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed before the game is released in app stores.

This policy explains what information the game iNardi, made by Each Pixel Matters ("we", "us"), collects, why, and what choices you have. Contact us any time at support@eachpixelmatters.com.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
A random player ID created when you first open the game (guest account). No name, email or phone number is required.To keep your coins, items and game progress, and to run online games.
Your player name (a random "Guest 1234" name until you change it).Shown to your opponents in online games.
Game data: coin balance and coin history, items you own and use, games played and won, the moves and dice of your online games.To run the game fairly, settle coin tables and prevent cheating.
Technical data such as IP address and basic device and browser information, recorded by our servers when the game connects.To deliver the service, keep it secure and fix problems.

The game also stores a few settings on your device (sound, chosen game type, menu scene).

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, location, camera or microphone.
  • Coins have no money value and cannot be cashed out or sent to other players.

Services we use

  • Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, EU) hosts our accounts and game database.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web version of the game and this website.
  • Google Fonts provides fonts for this website.
  • When the app is released with advertising and in-app purchases, Google AdMob and Google Play Billing (and their Apple equivalents) will be used. We will update this policy, and ask for consent where the law requires it, before that happens.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and game data while your account exists. Finished games may be kept for up to 12 months for fair-play checks and then deleted or anonymised. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, coins, items and coin history.

Your choices and rights

You can change your player name in the game at any time. You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your data: see Delete your account or write to support@eachpixelmatters.com. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU), you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

Children

iNardi is not directed at children under 13. Because the game includes coin tables, it is intended for players aged 13 and older, or the minimum age required where you live.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
