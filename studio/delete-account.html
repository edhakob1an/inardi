Delete your iNardi account

iNardi · by Each Pixel Matters

You can ask us to delete your iNardi account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Open iNardi and tap Profile. Note your player name.
  2. Send an email to support@eachpixelmatters.com with the subject Delete my iNardi account and your player name.
  3. We confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your player profile and name
  • Your coins, coin history and owned items
  • Your statistics

What may be kept

Records of finished online games may be kept without your name for up to 12 months for fair-play checks. Records of purchases may be kept as long as the law requires.

Deleting the account cannot be undone, and coins and items cannot be restored afterwards.
